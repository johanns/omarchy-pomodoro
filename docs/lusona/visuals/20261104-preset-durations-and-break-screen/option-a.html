Does this panel layout and break screen match what you want before implementation?

Decided here: the panel's control rows (focus presets, break length, three behavior toggles) and the full-screen break view. Out of scope: bar slot rendering, notification text, chime sound choice.

State

Binding

  • V1: Panel order top to bottom: hero (phase, status, countdown; no icon), progress, sessions, actions, Focus row, Break row, Behavior toggles.
  • V2: Focus row is one segmented control with exactly 15, 25, 50, 90; one selected at all times.
  • V3: Break row is one segmented control with Short (5 min) and Long (15 min); one selected at all times.
  • V4: Behavior holds exactly three toggles: Notification, Chime, Break screen.
  • V5: Break screen shows phase, countdown, progress, and three buttons in this order: Pause (Resume while paused), +5 min, Stop. No icon above the phase. A key hint line below the buttons names Esc as hide. It covers every monitor.
  • V7: Break screen progress is a row of dots across the width with a Pac-Man figure at the elapsed position; dots behind it are gone.
  • V6: Free-form minute fields and the "sessions per cycle" field are gone.

Illustrative

  • Colors, spacing, and fonts follow the active Omarchy theme at runtime.
  • Copy of captions and the break-screen meta line.
  • The session counter wording ("3 focus sessions done") while the cycle question is open.

Fidelity

Hand-drawn HTML of the real Quickshell panel. Production uses Omarchy kit components (ButtonGroup, Toggle, KeyboardPanel, OverlayWindow). The bar slot keeps its Nerd Font glyph; the panel and break screen show none. The break screen's wallpaper dimming and the Pac-Man drawing are approximate.

Open questions

  • Q1: With a manual Short/Long break choice, does the "long break every 4 sessions" cycle go away? The mockup assumes yes and shows a plain session counter.
  • Q2: Auto-start breaks and auto-start focus toggles are not in the three behavior options. Remove them, or keep them as settings without a toggle?
  • Q3: Break screen: should it also appear for a break started by Skip, and should it block input or stay dismissable with Esc or a click? Mockup: dismissable.
  • Q4: Chime sound: the freedesktop "complete" sound shipped on the system, or a bundled file?

Must not happen

  • The break screen must never trap the session: Esc, click, Hide, or Stop always removes it, and it never appears during a focus phase.
  • +5 min never pushes a break past 180 minutes left; it is ignored beyond that.
  • Changing the focus preset while a session runs must not reset the running countdown.
  • The chime must not play when the Chime toggle is off, and must never be routed through a shell string.
